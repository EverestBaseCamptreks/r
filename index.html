About High on Summit

High on Summit is Nepal's premier luxury trekking company, blending Himalayan expertise with world-class hospitality. Founded by veteran mountaineers, we curate journeys that prioritize safety, authentic cultural immersion, and unparalleled comfort. Every itinerary is designed for discerning travelers seeking transformative mountain experiences without compromise.

With a 100% safety record and certified guides, we offer exclusive permits, boutique teahouse upgrades, and private helicopter options. Your adventure, elevated.
